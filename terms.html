Website terms

The scope of this website and what joining the movement means.

Initial draft · Updated September 28, 2026

Purpose and status

Amanah Medical is a developing physician-led initiative. This website shares its proposed model and invites professional expressions of interest. Opportunities, partnerships, schedules, and services are subject to discussion and program readiness. These initial draft terms are provided transparently while the operating entity and jurisdiction are confirmed.

Joining is an introduction

A submitted form or welcome message acknowledges interest. It is not a credentialing decision, employment offer, clinical appointment, membership certification, or promise of placement. Clinical responsibilities, travel, compensation if any, licensing, and other obligations require separate arrangements before participation.

Accurate and appropriate submissions

Submit your own accurate professional information or information you are authorized to provide. Do not impersonate others, submit patient information, upload unlawful material, attempt unauthorized access, or abuse the forms. Public names are self-reported; Amanah may remove erroneous, abusive, or inappropriate directory entries.

No patient-care relationship

This site does not provide individual medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services. Viewing the site or submitting a form does not establish a clinician–patient relationship. For an emergency, use your local emergency service.

Content and external links

Website text, branding, physician portraits, and licensed stock photographs are used for this site. Do not assume they may be reused elsewhere. External sites have their own terms and privacy practices. The REIMAGE footer identifies the website service provider and is not a clinical endorsement.

Availability and separate agreements

We aim to keep information accurate, but content may change and service may be interrupted. Descriptions of intended programs are not guarantees of clinical or community outcomes. These terms do not remove rights that cannot legally be excluded; any future participation agreement will specify its own obligations and applicable law.

Contact

Questions about this website or a submission: info@amanahmedicalcare.com. See the privacy notice for information practices.

Hospital Partnerships

The proposed hub model

A regional hub hospital would work with volunteer physicians on consultation, training, and improvement priorities. The hub would also share learning with surrounding hospitals and clinics.

Local teams bring essential knowledge of their patients, systems, and resources. Collaboration begins with those priorities and aims to strengthen the capability already within the community.

Areas for collaboration

Clinical collaboration

Explore educational case discussions with physicians whose experience aligns with your team’s needs, subject to appropriate clinical and credentialing arrangements.

Teaching & training

Identify learning priorities and develop ways for your team to use new knowledge and share it with surrounding institutions.

Hospital improvement

Work together on clinical workflows, quality improvement priorities, and practical training approaches that fit your setting.

Scope and responsibilities

Physicians contribute their expertise voluntarily. Hospital partnership fees, services, and any reduced-rate arrangements are discussed during partnership planning.

Scope, responsibilities, local requirements, and knowledge-sharing with surrounding institutions would be agreed individually. Amanah is developing the initiative; an inquiry does not guarantee a partnership or service availability.

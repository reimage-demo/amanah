Where you could contribute

Bring your experience to the conversations, teams, and systems that shape patient care.

Roles, eligibility, scheduling, and any travel are discussed before a commitment. Opportunities depend on partner needs and program readiness.

Consultation

Join educational case discussions with local physicians. Share your reasoning and help colleagues explore complex clinical questions.

The aim is to support local clinical judgment and build confidence through collaboration. Clinical responsibilities must be agreed before participation.

Teaching and mentorship

Contribute teaching sessions, practical skills discussions, or mentorship shaped around the priorities of local teams.

The aim is learning that clinicians can apply in their own setting and share with nearby hospitals and clinics.

Hospital development

Collaborate with hospital leaders on clinical workflows, quality improvement, and training approaches.

The aim is practical, locally owned improvements that reflect available resources and the needs of the community.

An illustrative example

A specialist contributes to a case discussion with a hub hospital. The local team then incorporates the learning into training for nearby clinicians.

This is an example of the proposed model, not a report of an existing program or patient outcome.

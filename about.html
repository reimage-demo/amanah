About Amanah Medical

A physician-led initiative rooted in Islamic values of service and compassion, with a focus on strengthening healthcare capability within local communities.

Our purpose

Our mission is to connect physician expertise with healthcare teams in resource-limited settings, supporting the people who care for their communities every day.

Rooted in Islamic values of service and compassion, we see medical knowledge as a responsibility to share. Local clinicians are professional partners whose experience and priorities shape the work.

Amanah is developing its consultation, teaching, and hospital collaboration model. This recruitment initiative focuses on U.S. Muslim physicians and underserved communities in the Middle East. Opportunities and partnerships depend on readiness and local needs.

How inquiry information is used

Information submitted through our inquiry forms is used to respond to your inquiry and coordinate potential participation. Submissions are stored in Amanah’s database on Convex and are accessible to authorized Amanah administrators. If you explicitly opt in when joining, your name also appears in our public member directory. Your other answers remain private. See our privacy notice for details.

Please do not send patient information, medical records, license documents, or other sensitive clinical information. For questions about information you share, contact info@amanahmedicalcare.com.

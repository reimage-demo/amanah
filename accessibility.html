Accessibility statement

We want people to explore Amanah and express interest without unnecessary barriers.

Initial draft · Updated September 28, 2026

Our approach

We aim to support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, descriptive headings, labeled form fields, useful image descriptions, and clear form status messages. WCAG 2.2 Level AA is a design target, not a claim that the whole site has been independently certified or fully audited.

Current features and limits

The site includes a skip link, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, and labeled multistep forms. The directory and forms require JavaScript and an internet connection. Some external services and browser or assistive-technology combinations may behave differently. We have not completed a comprehensive assistive-technology audit.

Get help or report a barrier

Email info@amanahmedicalcare.com or call +1 505-218-7319. Tell us which page or task caused difficulty and, if helpful, your browser or assistive technology. Do not include patient information. You can contact the team directly to express interest if the form is not accessible to you.

Ongoing improvement

We will review reported barriers and update this statement as testing and improvements progress. This draft does not promise a response time or a conformance level that has not been verified.
